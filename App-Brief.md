## Who is this app for?

Maintenance or reliability engineer, or a business viewer, working at a desk on a desktop or laptop browser inside Cognite Fusion. They look up an asset, check its timeseries and documents, and add notes or upload documents to it; they do not edit the asset itself. (Assumed persona for an internal demo; no real user has been identified yet.)

## What problem does this solve?

To see what an asset or piece of equipment is, the viewer has to open several Fusion tools: a data model explorer to find assets and read their properties, a charting tool for its timeseries and the file browser for its documents. Assembling one asset picture takes many clicks and several tabs, links between an asset and its equipment are easy to miss, and there is no place to leave a note on the asset. (Written from how the app is built; the time cost has not been measured.)

## Tasks and success

**One-sentence story.** As an engineer or business viewer, I want to browse a register of assets and open one page per asset showing its properties, linked equipment, timeseries trends, notes and documents, where I can add a note or upload a document, so that I can understand and annotate it without switching between Fusion tools.

**Success criteria.** Demo target, not yet measured: a viewer finds an asset in the register, reads its properties and linked equipment, checks a timeseries trend and a document, and adds a note in under 3 minutes without leaving the app. For this first app, a successful draft deploy, signing and builder-certification run is also a success criterion.

**User evidence.** No user contact yet. This is an assumption based on the owner's own experience of the Fusion workflow. This is a first app built to learn the Cognite Flows deploy flow and to get builder certified.
