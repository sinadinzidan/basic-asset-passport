## Who is this app for?

Maintenance or reliability engineer, or a business viewer, working at a desk on a desktop or laptop browser inside Cognite Fusion. They look up an asset or piece of equipment but do not edit data. (Assumed persona for an internal demo; no real user has been identified yet.)

## What problem does this solve?

To see what an asset or piece of equipment is, the viewer has to open several Fusion tools: a data model explorer for properties and a file browser for datasheets and certificates. Assembling one equipment picture takes many clicks and several tabs, and links between an asset, its equipment and its documents are easy to miss. (Written from how the app is built; the time cost has not been measured.)

## Tasks and success

**One-sentence story.** As an engineer or business viewer, I want to open one read-only passport per piece of equipment showing its specifications and linked documents so that I can understand it without switching between Fusion tools.

**Success criteria.** Demo target, not yet measured: a viewer finds an equipment's specifications and a linked document from one screen in under 2 minutes without leaving the app. For this first app, a successful draft deploy, signing and builder-certification run is also a success criterion.

**User evidence.** No user contact yet. This is an assumption based on the owner's own experience of the Fusion workflow. This is a first app built to learn the Cognite Flows deploy flow and to get builder certified.
